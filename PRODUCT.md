# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile and desktop players who want short, readable survival-shooter runs with comfortable touch controls and persistent character progression.

## Product Purpose

Riftbreak Protocol is an original low-poly top-down survival shooter. Success means combat remains legible under heavy enemy counts, core actions feel immediate, and players can comfortably control the game in portrait or landscape orientation.

## Brand Personality

Tactical, energetic, industrial.

## Anti-references

Avoid copying the names, character likenesses, art, or branded presentation of the supplied reference game. Avoid generic fantasy ornament, excessive glass effects, and controls that prioritize decoration over readability.

## Design Principles

- Keep combat information readable at a glance.
- Make every touch control clear, responsive, and configurable.
- Preserve the compact field-terminal visual language across menus and gameplay.
- Use motion to communicate state, impact, and cooldowns.
- Keep all interactive targets at least 44 pixels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support portrait and landscape phone layouts, large touch targets, high-contrast state indicators, keyboard controls on desktop, and reduced reliance on color alone for cooldown or selection state.
